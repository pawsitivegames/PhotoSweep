# PhotoSweep icon candidate

Created with the built-in imagegen tool. Candidate assets are separate from the installed extension icons.

`photosweep-master.png` is the generated master with alpha. Extension-size exports: 16, 32, 48, 64, and 128 pixels.

Design: overlapping photo cards represent duplicate photos; a single sparkle communicates cleanup. Royal blue follows `lib/photo-sweep-colors.ts`.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final square app icon for PhotoSweep, a Chrome extension for reviewing duplicate photos and cleaning cloud photo libraries. Create one polished, original icon, not a presentation or mockup. Design: a bold royal blue #255BD4 rounded-square tile, two clearly overlapping photo cards in white and pale blue, with a very simple blue mountain silhouette and a small round sun in the front card, and ONE crisp four-point white cleanup sparkle near the upper-right of the cards. Communicate photos, duplicates, and careful cleanup. Use large simple shapes, generous negative space, clean vector-like edges, restrained subtle depth, strong contrast; readable at 16–48px. Center the mark, fill about 70% of the canvas, rounded square tile nearly fills canvas, transparent outside its rounded corners. Colors match app: royal blue #255BD4, dark blue #1948AD, white, pale blue #EDF3FF. No lettering, no text, no border decoration, no broom, trash can, shield, magnifier, arrows, extra sparkles, photographic textures, glossy 3D, watermark, or existing provider logos. Output one square 1024x1024 icon with transparent corner areas.
